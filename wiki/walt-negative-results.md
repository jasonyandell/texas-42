## Why this page exists

Most of what walt has established is negative, and that is by design rather than
by accident. Every probe in the program was specified so that both outcomes would
be reportable before it ran — the standing formulation is Jason's, that if the
whole thing falls on its face that is fine — and the **no-rescue policy** of
2026-08-10 makes the disposition of a failure explicit: a failure is a
counterexample to carry back to the mathematics, never a thing to engineer
around, spin, or assist past. A negative reached under a pre-declared criterion
is a measurement; a negative quietly reinterpreted afterwards is nothing at all.

So the negatives are collected here as results, with their attributions intact.
Each entry states the hypothesis, the pre-declared criterion where one was
declared, the verdict with its number, where the number lives (record path and
gate file), the scope the verdict binds at, and what the program did next. The
sessions that produced them are narrated in the era pages; this page is the
cross-era index, in program order: the research eras (2026-08-09 → 08-16), then
the player eras (2026-08-24 → 09-07).

## The compression negatives

These four are one family. They are the successive answers to a single question:
is there a lawful notion of "the same situation" small enough to carry a seat's
decision?

### The only lumpable skeletons were world-reconstructing (S4)

**Hypothesis.** A descriptor coarser than the world exists whose update is closed
over observations and whose classes preserve the response.

**Verdict.** At the S4 candidate-space size, the only skeletons passing both
checkers exhaustively were the world-reconstructing ones; every strictly coarser
candidate lost predictive sufficiency. What *did* compress was
history-forgetting rather than state-coarsening — remembering who holds what
while forgetting who played what and in which order collapsed 5,887 nodes to
2,857 classes across the 13 trick-six kernels, and adding beater counts changed
no class at all, because they are a function of the holder map.

**Scope and sequel.** Candidate-space-relative, on 13 trick-six kernels. Two
later findings reinterpret it. S4.5 showed the *soundness* ceilings that came
with it were vocabulary rather than physics: the rescued exp3A control atoms
broke every ceiling the holder registry had hit. And S5d found the deeper cause
of the lumpability side — the S4 instantiation compared observation and feature
alphabets **raw**, so only a descriptor that reconstructed the world could match
them. That diagnosis is what produced §12.6A. Details:
[foundation era](walt-foundation-era.md), [factory era](walt-factory-era.md).
Producer: `walt-skeleton`, archive-only at commit `648f93a`.

### Structural matching compresses nothing where the decisions are (S5e, S5f)

**Hypothesis.** A structural relabeling quotient over situations merges enough of
them to bring the count down.

**Verdict.** The finest structural quotient passes the equivariant lumpability
condition exhaustively — the v0.5 existence question resolves YES — and merges
**zero of 647 roots**. Every declared structural coarsening stayed at 647/647 as
well, with a dividend of exactly 1.000 at the first three plies. Only the
*retrograde* quotient, which identifies situations by their future dynamics
rather than by structural matching, compressed at all: 15,253 situations to 1,459
classes and roots 647 to 306. Climbing one trick earlier then weakened even that:
root compression fell from 2.1:1 at trick six to 1.25:1 at trick five (16,112
roots → 12,924 classes), and the trick-five inventory was nowhere near converged
at 13 hands.

**Scope and sequel.** Carrier-relative; classes named by future cones, with no
compact-description claim attached. The extrapolation — world-level trick-1 roots
running astronomically past any bar — is what redirected the program from the
world-level census to the seat-level one. Records:
`walt/probes/factory-results/census_2026-08-10{,_r2,_r3}.txt`,
`census_t5_2026-08-10.txt`. Details: [census era](walt-census-era.md).

### The seat's first-play quotient is the identity (S5k)

**Hypothesis.** At the first play, lawful equivalence collapses the seat's
situation space to order 10^5.

**Verdict.** Refuted by proof rather than by measurement. Corollary S-rigid: the
pip-trump structure on all 28 live tiles admits no nontrivial self-transport, and
fixing the focal seat kills rotation, so the seat-side structural quotient at the
first play is the **identity**. The count is therefore exactly
C(28,7) = 1,184,040, about 11.84× over the declared bar, with the only available
symmetry the exact 7:1 declaration fold of Lemma S-fold. Lemma S-det adds that
there is no compression at the first-trick interface either.

**What it taught, and this is the reframing of the whole program:** structural
compression is bought with **deadness** — dead tiles, inert contexts — which is
why one measured endgame level collapses 55,036,800 states to 32,532 canonical
forms to 64 classes, and why nothing merges at the first play, where nothing is
dead yet. The identity quotient is not a failure of technique; it is the
statement that the abstraction was too fine for the question. Jason's recorded
reaction is part of the result: he was thrilled by it, as proof that the existing
objects do not represent the thing he is after, and therefore as an opportunity.
Whether a coarser lawful equivalence — dynamics-style or value-partition-style —
reaches 10^5 is named OPEN. The receipt build was parked (no `seat_census`
results file exists; freezes 18–21 spent). Statements and provenance:
[structure and transport](walt-math-structure-transport.md); rulings S-A1..S-A21.

### Linear predictive compression saturates (S6a, Gate B)

**Hypothesis.** Exact predictive rank over the rationals compresses the seat's
state where the partition quotient could not, because linear rank can be far
below partition-lump size.

**Verdict.** Refuted at the pre-declared thresholds. Two parts. First, by
adjudication rather than measurement: Lemma R(c) observes that in straight 42
every tile is eventually played and publicly attributed, so a complete
continuation record determines the latent world — hence any closure whose
terminal seed carries a nonzero constant has predictive dimension exactly |X|,
and the distribution contracts were theorems before a line of code ran. That left
exactly one measurable object, the count-free expected-trick value closure.
Second, the measurement: dim V^val came out 1, then 42–59, then 1461–1680 at
grades 1, 2 and 3 against |X| = 6, 90 and 1,680 — one grade-3 coordinate at
**full rank**. The pre-declared criterion compared dimension growth to fiber
growth: D(2)/D(1) = 59 against a fiber ratio of 15, D(3)/D(2) = 28.5 against
18.7 — the dimension grows at least as fast as the fiber at both steps.

**Scope and sequel.** Coordinate-relative, under one declared contract, field and
observation model. Explicitly *not* killed by it: root-action argmax partitions,
the dual policy geometry, and moment compilation for fixed shallow queries. The
mechanism is worth carrying — the field-share weights are world-discriminating
even with the constant excluded — because it is the same public-attribution
structure that killed the partition quotient. Two different compression programs,
one structural cause. Record: `predictive_rank_2026-08-12.txt`; rulings
R-A1..R-A24. Details: [S6 era](walt-s6-era.md).

## The performance negatives

These three are the answers to "does this clever object make the seat's solve
cheaper?" All three are no, and all three left their machinery standing.

### The class DAG is not a first-build accelerator (S5h)

**Hypothesis.** Evaluating a coordinate's fiber over the retrograde class DAG
beats evaluating it directly.

**Verdict.** It loses, at every rung, to the honest control. The rulings had
already rejected the originally proposed comparison as a strawman (P-Q2): the
fair control is not a plain tree walk but the ordinary transposition cache the
project already had. Against that control the class DAG runs about 4.0 to 5.0
times *slower* at first build (B:A1 per-coordinate 4.036–5.041; rung medians
4.737 / 4.297 / 4.903). Meanwhile the control itself is the real prize —
ordinary memoisation beats the plain tree by roughly 6× at four tricks remaining
and 100× at six (A1:A0 wall medians 0.166, 0.024, 0.010), and the dividend
compounds with depth.

**The reason is structural, not an implementation accident:** a class identity is
a function of the future cone, so it is computable only after full expansion.
Retrograde identity cannot short-circuit descent the way a state key can. The
standing conclusion is that **the class store is a storage and transport object**
— reuse across coordinates, hands and weightings — and never a first-build
accelerator. Interior collapse is real regardless (1,502,362 situations to
128,860 classes at one coordinate); the inventory compresses, the build cost does
not. The weighted re-solve over a fixed class DAG — the number the platform
claim rests on — remains UNMEASURED. Records: `fiber_probe_2026-08-11.txt`,
`fiber_probe_h_2026-08-11.txt`; rulings P-A1..P-A21.

### The endgame tablebase loses at evaluation (S5j)

**Hypothesis.** Precomputing the cheap end of the game and canonicalizing
positions by structural form gives lookups that beat solving.

**Verdict.** The tablebase arms run 1.57 to 2.69 times slower than the plain
cache at every one of 17 coordinates, and the attribution is unambiguous:
canonicalization dominates, at roughly 4.6 microseconds per form against a
state-key probe two orders of magnitude cheaper, and under a memo the subtree a
hit saves is already collapsed, so the per-hit saving is smaller than the
per-state price of finding it. The floor table is a 41× negative in its own
right — 1,430 nanoseconds for a lookup against 35 nanoseconds for closed-form
last-trick resolution — and is reported as one.

**But the convergence being chased is real**, which is why this is a finding
about *where* to spend it rather than about whether it exists: 830,399 form-hits
across the traversal, an aggregate rate near 38% and reaching 73% at one hand —
relabeling-symmetric repeats that a state key cannot see. The one arm that beat
the control was the closed-form bottom, at 0.88–0.99 of it. The complete
level-one form count, 32,532 distinct canonical forms against a 64-class
alphabet, became the first direct size data for the seat-level census question.

**Read with S5h, the pair is a clean lesson:** cone identity cannot short-circuit
descent; structural identity *can*, but harvesting it costs a canonicalization
per distinct state, and at this depth that price exceeds the harvest. Symmetry
pays where the per-hit saving is large, where the form is cheap, or where the
form inventory is itself the object of interest. Records:
`endgame_store_2026-08-11.txt`, `endgame_floor_2026-08-11.txt`; rulings
E-A1..E-A21.

### The exclusion engine works and does not bite (S5i)

**Hypothesis.** Refining a fiber by declared exclusions — everything that could
happen except X — yields smaller fibers and cheaper evaluation.

**Verdict.** Split. The mathematics landed: Lemma X establishes that deleting
worlds of value zero leaves both the unnormalised objective and its argmax
exactly unchanged, one-sided. The economics landed too — a predicate pass over a
built store costs a fraction of a millisecond (0.1–3.7 ms) against multi-second
builds, and for the value predicates the cheapest storeless alternative costs
hundreds of milliseconds (197–958 ms), so the multi-pass route is orders of
magnitude cheaper than its best competitor, while reachability and confinement
predicates have no storeless alternative at all. What did **not** land is the
bite: on the measured coordinates the declared exclusions flag 0.1–3.5% of
classes but **0 of 240 evaluated worlds**, because a world of value zero requires
losing every remaining trick under world-informed play and the corpus focal seat
is the declarer. And exclusion saves nothing at evaluation time once the store is
paid.

**Scope and sequel.** The engine is proven either way and Lemma X stands as a
theorem regardless of bite. Where bite might live is named and explicitly not
claimed: count-bearing exclusions after role re-entry, non-declarer focal seats,
and the seat's real voided support. One durable distinction came out of it —
remnants are **analyst conditioning**, a third thing beside support and belief:
excluded means neither impossible nor improbable, and one never re-optimises over
a remnant and calls the result a seat value. Record: `fiber_refine_2026-08-11.txt`;
rulings X-A1..X-A19.

## The discipline negatives

These are places where walt's own honesty machinery caught walt.

### The lesson inventory was label-fragile after all (S5c-m2 versus m3)

Everything the factory measured ran at the omniscient label. Re-measuring at the
seat's actual label was declared the inventory's weakest load-bearing assumption
and tested directly. The first pass returned 10 survive, 0 fail, 5 unmeasured,
and concluded the inventory was not label-fragile — but four of the five
unmeasured lessons were budget-capped on big fibers. When the memoized solver
lifted the caps, three of those four **failed** at the seat's label. An uncapped,
unmemoized tree walk reproduced every per-action value byte-identically (tree
steps 15,486,288,612 / 10,766,263,412 / 4,214,899,874 / 65,449,828,676 against
the DAG's 123,882,398 / 226,094,450 / 226,613,736 / 537,862,903), so the
failures are real label fragility, localized to the big early-trick fibers where
the omniscient and seat-facing fields diverge, and not an artifact of
memoisation.

The correction is the result: the first conclusion was **scale-limited**, and the
label-relativity caveat that had been riding every basin line was the truth.
Records: `label_transfer_2026-08-10_r3.txt`, `h_tree_crossval_2026-08-10.txt`.
Details: [factory era](walt-factory-era.md).

### Lesson basins were tiny (S5b), then the direction survived a sharpened test (S5c-m1)

The first measured generalization run produced refutation basins with a median of
zero to one decision. Reported as a falsification pressure rather than smoothed
over, it caused the next milestone to be re-scoped to *be* the falsification
test, with relaxable bound pairs and cut refinement added. On that sharpened
test the direction survived: implicants generalized into pure atom cells that
transferred across hands and paid measurable purpose-specific rent. The honest
summary of the pair is that the mechanism works and the **scale** is the standing
problem. Records: `lesson_basins_2026-08-10.txt`, `falsification_2026-08-10{,_r2}.txt`.

### A shape notion that was vacuous, caught in-run (S5g)

The first shape notion proved arity-blind across levels — and was flagged in-run
as vacuous, carried back, and never reinterpreted as a positive. The leak was
systemic: every unconstrained node has full-hand arity by rule. The hereditary
rung then proved degenerate for a structural reason of its own. What survived was
the parts catalog: on the one clean step, the suffix library grew 31.558× against
class growth of 368.625× with overlap exactly 1.000, so classes are menus over a
compact shared catalog of parts. Records: `census_yard_2026-08-10.txt`,
`census_yard_v2_2026-08-10.txt`.

### A capped probe reports no verdict (S6b)

Seven of nine measured grade-3 pairs collapse to a singleton Pareto frontier —
one policy weakly dominating every lawful alternative in all 1,680 worlds,
against raw plan counts as large as 2^19930. The other two blew past the declared
frontier cap of 16,384. Under the pre-declared discipline a partial frontier
bounds nothing, so **the formal verdict is STOPPED, with no verdict** (PG-A13),
and it is never presented as a seven-of-nine success. The bimodality is the
finding: total strategy-side collapse almost everywhere, genuine explosion
exactly where the 42 is tense.

That negative was then partly dissolved from the other side. S6d certified the
root action at the very coordinate whose frontiers could not be completed —
proving the frontier unnecessary for the root decision, which is the
decision-sparse thesis in one measured instance, and fenced explicitly as *not*
the parent's economy claim (SEP-A15(ii)). Records: `policy_geometry_2026-08-12.txt`,
`separation_2026-08-13.txt`.

### A detector that never fired, and a mechanism nobody can explain (S6c)

Of three proved detectors, one (D1-win) never fired at all across 45 units: its
sweep condition does not occur at these coordinates. And one identifiable family
of positions — the trumpless-junk grade-3 family — carries hundreds of ties per
lead (276 to 1,773) with zero detector hits. Its tie mechanism is
**unidentified** and stays a named open question; the standing rule is that no
fourth detector gets written without a proof first (J-A8). Related: the reported
recall figure (about 33%) understates the detectors, because the ground truth
they are scored against is one-deviation indifference, a superset of the strict
deadness they are proved to certify. Records: `deadness_2026-08-12.txt`,
`deadness_rung_2026-08-13.txt` (the sequential timing rung was run: 17 ns/call
at the grade-3 unit, 42 ns/call at the n = 4 unit, W = 1).

### The n = 4 gate said NO-GO, and the gate failure was filed as a result (S6f)

**Hypothesis.** Under the freeze-44 walk-step budgets a full n = 4 separation
rung is affordable: the partition build stays under P_max and every unit fits

**Criterion, declared in advance.** A single-arm cost-model rung at coordinate

against 4 × 10^10 walk-steps, P_max = 32,000,000 states and a 600,000 ms unit

**Verdict: NO-GO.** The U side is affordable (estimated whole-fiber revealed cost
4,327,256,587 walk-steps against the 4 × 10^10 budget), but the partition build
STOPPED at the 10^10 walk-step cap (`cap_hit true`), the estimated h0 unit wall
was 800,884 ms and the largest unit (h9) 3,482,961 ms against 600,000, and the
fallback failed its own arithmetic at h8. The blocking regression (R0) passed:
the grade-3 receipt reproduced under the budgeted evaluators. Record:
`walt/probes/factory-results/separation_n4_rung_2026-08-14.txt` (read in full;
a cost-model input, no ratio, no dividend).

**A disagreement to carry, not resolve here.** The results file prints the

**What it rules out, and what it does not.** It rules out the v1 budgets, not

P_max v2 = 192,000,000, and S6h then reached Tier 1 at all nine real-deal
coordinates — 4 SEPARATED, 4 EXACT NEGATIVES (Corollary E4.1(3): no candidate

### The bounded two-sided witness at trick 1 was refuted by proof (S6g)

**Hypothesis.** A hand-only lower bound (the focal seat's top trump run, Lemma
T1-run) against an opponents'-forced-tricks upper bound (Lemma T1-force) closes
at trick 1 without a solve — the parent's "bounded sandwich", CBS-A3's list of
retired names records it as REFUTED.

**Verdict, before any compute.** Two delivered propositions. **T1-blind**: a
lower witness valid at every root action (L ≤ Q^H(a) for all a) can never
strictly exclude a competitor, since U_a ≥ Q^H(a) ≥ L — "the strongest verdict
such a pair can produce is membership certified simultaneously for every action,
which distinguishes a⋆ from nothing"; a non-trivial witness must be false for the
competitors. **T1-corner**: with L = 2k − 7 and U = 7 − 2·E_β[f], L ≥ U iff
k + E_β[f] ≥ 7, which holds iff k = 7 — the focal seat holds the entire trump
suit. The proposition's later twin on the upper side is FT-flat (an action-blind
upper feature can never shave).

**What replaced it.** Theorem T1-draw: on a closed family of 294 declared
trick-1 coordinates the focal seat takes all seven tricks against every field
behaviour in every world — 7 coordinates TRIVIAL (all-trump), **287 with Opt^H
determined exactly by exhaustive integer counts over all 399,072,960 worlds**, no
decimation; flagship PipTrump(6) hand {6:6 6:5 6:4 6:3 6:2 6:1 5:5}:
Q^H(5:5) = 7 − 143/5814 exactly, member-not-set DISCHARGED. Corpus arm: none of
the 13 receipt trick-1 hands draws (exact corner gaps 4 to 19/3; the results
file governs over the LOG's 92/15). "A drawing hand is a hand that plays
itself"; the theorem says nothing about hands that require judgement. Record:
`trick1_draw_2026-08-14.txt` (2,891 lines, 159,678 ms); rulings T1-A1..T1-A12;
freeze 47. Risk T1-A12 carried: proved relative to `rules.rs`.

**Criterion.** Oracle-θ capture (a ratio of two taxes, convention-free) over the

**Verdict.** Over h0's 574 leading states F1 captures
88457474377/24082518161460 ≈ **3,673 parts per million** — about a third of one
percent — and exactly 0 over the 758 following states (overall 3,570–3,571 ppm);
elsewhere the feature is inapplicable, because a boss-keyed feature's domain
shrinks as the hand simplifies. On the same 574 states the sibling beatability
feature F2 captures 76.4628% (2841944614/3716765745) — the two candidates differ
by about 208× — and exactly 0 over h2's 216 swept states with 3,126 breakpoints
each, a genuine refutation, later diagnosed (S6n) as tie-driven. Six of twelve
cells of the first run were vacuous by a freeze-text defect and are typed
UNMEASURED, not zero. Records: `feature_fee_2026-08-14.txt` (F1 at line 1404–1408),
`feature_fee_v11_2026-08-14.txt`; rulings FF-A1..FF-A33; freeze 52 v1 → v1.4.

**What it rules out, and what it does not.** F1 as a fee that bites at these

for them.

**Verdict.** The first carrier not selected by outcome — 100 seeds by

**What it rules out, and what it does not.** The multiplicity → separation
arrow of the fee-screening chain at unselected coordinates; the h2/h0 fee

## The sampling and evidence negatives (2026-08-24 to 2026-08-25)

### The shadow instrument never eliminated the live line at cap 128, and the "~108/116 settle by 512" proxy was retracted

**Hypothesis.** Running the adaptive controller beside the live level-1 player
would settle many of its decisions, and the 128-epoch refinement vectors

**Verdict.** At world cap 128 (33 hands, 183 decisions; 13 receipt + 20 driven):
ExactFrozenSet 67, **Unresolved 116, DeltaSettled 0**; the live choice was among
the survivors 116/116; every trick-1 to trick-3 decision Unresolved (33/33/29);

## What the negatives bought

Read together, the refutations narrow the search far more sharply than the
positives do.

- Two independent compression programs died of the **same structural cause** —
  the game's public-attribution observation structure, which makes complete
  records determine worlds. That is a fact about 42, not about either technique.
- Compression is available **late and not early**, because it is bought with
  deadness. Any plan that needs the seat's opening situation space to be small
  has to buy smallness some other way.
- Retrograde and structural identity are **transport objects, not accelerators**.
  Their value, if any, is reuse across coordinates, hands and weightings — which
  is untested, and is a different claim from the one that was refuted.
- Ordinary transposition memoisation, the least exotic instrument in the
  workspace, is the one that actually pays, and it compounds with depth.
- The decision side of 42 collapses far more than the value side does. That is
  what redirected the program from compressing truth to **proving the root
  action** — and, by way of the two-sided witness, to root intervals, certified
  regret and the focal-horizon hierarchy.
- An action-blind bound can never exclude a competitor (T1-blind, FT-flat): every
  later lower witness is a materialized policy and every later upper is
  action-conditioned.
- **The modeled minds are the bill.** Sampling more, sharing more and bundling
  more bought about 4%, 3% and 1.04× on the convicted regime; what moved the
  program was counting exactly instead of sampling, and then paying for σ0
  reads only where a focal decision is made exact.
- At every root measured, once one focal layer is exact the width left is the
  **tail's policy gap, not the fusion price**, and a ply cut can be 1‰ wrong in
  value and flip the play — so a better lawful tail is worth more than a deeper
  search, and no cut is ever a verdict.
- The 6-4 problem is not a modelling depth problem: at exact indifference pmake
  has no gradient and the play falls out of a tile-index tie-break. The levers
  are the objective and the tie-break.
- Partner modelling at the budgets tried buys nothing measurable against the
  phone or against level 1, per-root exact tables do not generalize, shared
  relational rules trail them, and prices that shift every action by the same
  constant cannot rerank anything. What survives is the instrument — an
  expression language that can write a situation down and an exact gym that can
  grade it.
