## The vocabulary, because it is typed and not decorative (DS-A1)

- **primal witness** — L_a, the exact value of a **fixed** lawful
  information-consistent policy, integrated under the declared belief and field.
- **upper witness** — U_a, the action-conditioned relaxation value.
- **root-action separation** — the relation L_{a⋆} ≥ U_a for every a ≠ a⋆.
- **receipt** — a machine-checked verification artifact regenerated by a run.

**The word "certificate" is not used in walt artifacts written under this
ruling** (D3). Quotations of the received documents that use it are bracketed.
The ruling binds **forward** from 2026-08-13, so a grep of `walt/` returns
pre-ruling and received-document hits that are not violations; see
[the reference map](walt-math-reference.md#standing-disciplines-a-successor-must-not-relearn-the-hard-way)
for what the operative test actually is.

## 1. Order exchange

### Theorem E1 — order exchange under a declared involution (§1.3; DS-A6)

**Repairs parent §7.1, which is UNSOUND as written.** Two defects were proved:
under a literal reading the two kernels have disjoint support in the trace
coordinate, so the hypothesis holds for no pair of distinct blocks and the
statement is **vacuous**; and under the intended transport reading the proof
step "compose both sides with the same lawful successor policy" is invalid,
because under perfect recall the continuation is a function of the trace and the
two traces are related by the transport, not equal.

**Hypotheses.** **(H1)** Θ is a declared bijection exchanging the two blocks'
trace labels. **(H2) Intertwining:** 𝖪_b ⋆ 𝖪_a = Θ_*(𝖪_a ⋆ 𝖪_b) as kernels on
(trace, accumulated outcome, successor latent). **(H3) Θ-closure of the policy
class:** π ∘ Θ is lawful whenever π is, and conversely.

**Conclusion.** For every latent state, the **set** of achievable terminal
outcome laws is the same under both orders; hence the same **optimal**
continuation value for every utility readable from the preserved contract and
every belief.

**The three load-bearing clauses, none droppable.**

1. The conclusion is about the achievable **set and the optimum**, not about a
   fixed continuation policy. Equality for each fixed continuation is
   recoverable only by restricting to interface-measurable policies — a strictly
   smaller class than treatment H's perfect-recall class — and a design using
   that form must declare the restriction, whose optimum is then not Q^H.
2. **(H3) is a hypothesis about the policy class, not a consequence of (H2).**
   It holds automatically when Θ is induced by a relabeling of the game itself,
   and must be proved otherwise.
3. The intertwining is checked against the **still-leadable structure only**.
   Contexts that can never be led again are inert and erasable; checking against
   inert structure costs recall, never soundness.

**The outcome monoid, correctly located (§1.5).** (H2) forces the joint law of
the accumulated outcome to agree, hence forces the emitted increments to commute:
commutativity is a **necessary condition for (H2) to be satisfiable**, not an
extra hypothesis of the proof. Both declared contracts are commutative, so
commutativity is not where the count boundary bites. The count obstruction is
sharper: in the decorated kernel the increment includes the point value of the
tile focal played, so if the blocks play tiles of different point values the
*decorated* intertwining fails even when its count-free shadow holds.

### Corollaries E1.1 and E1.2 (§1.6)

- **E1.1.** In the Proposition J-1 instance — Θ induced by the transposition
  (t₁ t₂) — hypotheses (H1)–(H3) all hold, because τ is an automorphism of the
  remaining extensive game fixing every non-focal holding.
- **E1.2 (count survival under the guard).** If focal's hand contains no count
  tile, (H2) holds in the count-decorated monoid and the conclusion holds for
  trick-plus-count, because τ moves only t₁ and t₂ and the decoration is
  preserved exactly when c(t₁) = c(t₂).

**Theorem E1 is strictly more general than E1.1**: it requires only the
two-block kernel identity up to Θ, not a full automorphism. That generality is
the content of promoting D1 to a trace-equivalence layer, and it is available
only with (H1)–(H3) discharged.

### Theorem E1′ — order exchange under a fully transported involution (§8.1; DS-A19)

Generalises E1 to bijections (Θ_T on traces, Θ_M a monoid automorphism on
outcomes, Θ_X on successor latents), adding two hypotheses the proposal did not
name and which are **not decoration**:

- **(H4′) continuation equivariance** — transporting successor states without
  transporting the process that runs from them is not enough; the composed law
  then fails to correspond.
- **(H5′) utility invariance under Θ_M** — vacuous for the count-free contract;
  for the count-decorated monoid it demands the exchanged tiles carry equal
  point values, **which is exactly what the no-count guard supplies**.

Theorem E1 is the Θ_M = Θ_X = id case. **(H5′) is the same condition as Lemma
E8's valuation restriction seen from the kernel side** — the generalised theorem
and the cone correction are one fact in two coordinates.

## 2. The advantage dimension (§2; DS-A3, DS-A4)

**Definition E2 (binding form).** d_adv(E) = dim span{α − α′ : α, α′ ∈ E} — the
dimension of the direction space of the affine hull. Reference-free.

**Proposition E2.1.** For the parent's reference form d(E; α₀), we have
d(E; α₀) = d_adv(E) if α₀ ∈ aff(E), and d_adv(E) + **1** otherwise.

**Why it matters.** The parent's claim "a singleton envelope has d_adv = 0"
holds under the reference form only when the reference *is* that singleton, and
fails by exactly one otherwise — which is precisely the cross-action comparison
the parent recommends. Under Definition E2 the claim is unconditional:
d_adv(E) = 0 ⟺ |E| = 1. The repair concerns only the dimension; the baseline
value is still retained for absolute V and Q.

## 3–4. The two witnesses, and the way one of them inverts

### Lemma E3 — validity of the action-conditioned upper witness (§3.2; DS-A7)

Define V*_a(ξ) as the value in world ξ when focal plays a at the root and
thereafter chooses **with full knowledge of ξ**, against the same fixed field —
treatment C with the root action held. Set **U_a = E_β[V*_a]**. Then for every
information-consistent ρ with root action a and every ξ, α_ρ(ξ) ≤ V*_a(ξ) — the
pointwise strategy-fusion inequality — and consequently

> **Q^H_B(a) ≤ U_a.**

**Naming clause (DS-A20).** Treatment **C** reveals the physical world ω only.
Revealing an augmented latent (ω, z) — a persistent field state, policy type or
random tape — is a strictly larger relaxation; still a valid upper witness by
the same proof, but written **C⁺** and never called C unqualified. On the
measured carrier ξ = ω and the two coincide.

### Remark E3.1 — the unconditioned aggregate is valid but action-constant (§3.3)

With U^agg = E_β[max_a V*_a] we have Q^H(a) ≤ U_a ≤ U^agg for every a. So U^agg
is a valid upper witness for every action — **using it is not unsound** — but it
does not depend on a, so the separation test degenerates to L_{a⋆} ≥ U^agg,
demanding that a single lawful policy match the full strategy-fusion bound. It
is therefore sound and, in general, **vacuous**. The branch's measured
U^agg − V^H is a root-level quantity and is **not** a per-action information
price.

### The four attached conditions (§3.4)

U_a is an upper witness only when, jointly with the primal witness it is
compared against: **(C1)** the same fixed field on both sides; **(C2)** the same
belief and the **same world set** — in particular **no decimation may appear
inside L or U**, since a sampled mean is neither a lower nor an upper bound, and
a separation built on one is void (decimation remains lawful for choosing
*which* coordinates to certify); **(C3)** the same utility and count contract;
**(C4)** perfect-information minimax is **not** a substitute — a different
operator, and not a valid upper bound for a fixed stochastic field.

### Lemma E4 — validity of a primal witness (§4.1; DS-A14)

For an information-consistent ρ evaluated by the **fixed-policy evaluator**
α_ρ(ξ) = E[U | ξ, ρ, field] — containing no maximisation at any node below the
root — and any finite candidate set C_a:

> **L_a = max_{ρ ∈ C_a} ⟨β, α_ρ⟩ ≤ Q^H_B(a)**, with equality iff some candidate
> attains the maximum over the whole lawful class.

The proof is one line; the content is entirely in the two hypotheses —
information-consistency and fixed-policy evaluation. **Both are load-bearing,
and the second fails silently.**

### Non-theorem E4′ — the inversion failure mode (§4.2)

If L is computed with **any** evaluator that maximises inside the world after
conditioning on ξ — a world-informed rollout, treatment C or F, a revealed-world
solver, an unconditioned aggregate, a per-world best response — then by Lemma E3
it is an **upper** bound, the chain breaks at its first inequality, and the test
can separate an action that is not optimal **while every displayed step still
typechecks**.

**A minimal witness that the failure is real.** Two worlds with uniform β; after
a⋆ the focal seat cannot distinguish them and has two continuations with world
values (1,0) and (0,1). Every information-consistent policy must choose one at
the single information state, so Q^H(a⋆) = 1/2, while the world-informed price
is 1. If a competitor has U_a = 3/4 ≥ Q^H(a), installing 1 as L separates a⋆ by
1 ≥ 3/4, while in truth Q^H(a⋆) = 1/2 < Q^H(a) is possible. **The separated
action can be strictly worse than the rejected one.**

**The obligation (restated semantically under DS-A27).** The binding invariant
is semantic: *every later focal action is supplied by the candidate policy, and
no optimiser selects a focal action using hidden-state information.* Nonfocal
expectation, chance summation, deterministic singleton choices and
implementation maxima over singleton sets do not violate it. **"No max node
below the root" remains an accepted sufficient implementation form — cheap to
assert structurally and impossible to game — but it is a receipt for the
invariant, not the invariant.** Either way the assertion is structural, not by
inspection, and every run asserts L ≤ Q^H wherever treatment H completes.

### Corollary E4.1 — the primal ceiling, and the exact negative

*Filed in `CENSUS-RULINGS.md` § "Experiment E adjudication" with full proof; to
be filed as errata §4.3 at the next errata amendment, per DS-A28(ii).*

1. **(Ceiling.)** L_a ≤ Q^H_B(a) for every candidate set, with equality iff some
   candidate attains the lawful maximum.
2. **(Attainment.)** If the H operator is exact backward induction on the
   information tree and π_a assigns to every state **any** action attaining the
   max, then π_a is lawful and ⟨β, α_{π_a}⟩ = Q^H_B(a). So an H-argmax-seeded
   candidate set gives L_a = Q^H_B(a) **for every tie-breaking rule**.
3. **(The exact negative.)** If Q^H_B(a⋆) < U_a for some competitor, then **no
   finite candidate set whatsoever** separates a⋆ from a under that relaxation
   at that coordinate. The remaining lever is a **tighter relaxation** — a
   gluing cut — and never a better candidate.

**What it buys and what it costs.** It buys the sharpest primal witness
available, so a separation failure is maximally informative. It costs the
experiment its primal content: L is no longer a measurement of the candidate
library but a receipt tying two evaluators together.

### Corollary E3.2 — the zero-global-gap corollary (§8.2; DS-A22)

If U^agg = V^H then for **every** H-optimal root action, U_{a⋆} = Q^H(a⋆) = V^H,
and U_a ≤ V^H for every action. Consequently, if a primal witness attains V^H,
separation certifies membership **with no gluing iteration at all**: at a
zero-fusion-gap coordinate the entire remaining difficulty is **primal**.

**Three non-implications, and they matter.** It does **not** imply (i) that U_a
is tight for a suboptimal a — it bounds U_a by V^H and says nothing about
Q^H(a); (ii) that the separation is strict — every inequality it supplies is
non-strict, so it certifies membership and never uniqueness; (iii) that a primal
witness attaining V^H has been found — the corollary supplies no witness, and
finding one is the whole primal problem.

## 5. The Scheme-mass closure is degenerate in this game (§5; DS-A5, DS-A20, DS-A21)

**Proposition E5.** Under the parent §10.3 hypotheses imposed at **every
reachable interface**, a field of full support on legal plays, and **(S) latent
separation by complete records**, the algebra at every interface separates the
points of its latent domain: its atoms are singletons and the dimension is
exactly |X_i|. The proof shows the closure contains ξ ↦ Pr_ξ(complete record),
and a complete record names every hidden tile with its seat.

**Lemma E5.0 (§5.1a; DS-A20).** (S) **holds on the measured carrier** — the
latent is exactly the hidden current remainder, the field memoryless
uniform-legal — by the naming argument, with Pr_·(r) = w_r·1[· = ξ_r], w_r > 0.
So Proposition E5's conclusion stands **unconditionally** for every measurement
the branch has taken. (S) is future-proofing against an augmented latent, where
the argument would yield indicators of complete-record *classes* rather than
singletons — a weaker and possibly compressive conclusion, and then worth
measuring.

**Corollary E5.1.** No compression claim may be read from parent §10.3 in this
game. A reported atom count below |X| means a hypothesis was not imposed at
every reachable interface; the algebra is then **purpose-relative** and must be
declared as such, with the purpose named.

**Corollary E5.2 (language narrowed under DS-A21).** The fixed-policy evaluation
claim survives in purpose-relative form — that is exactly Lemma R's value
closure, which never admits the constant and therefore escapes the argument.
What forces the degeneracy is exactly the constant: exact **normalised**
filtering needs Pre(1) at every step. The binding negative is

> **"atom-mass *linear* filtering is noncompressive on this carrier"**

and **not** "filtering and compression are incompatible" unqualified. E5 rules
out a small universal atom algebra closed under complete-record residuals. It
does **not** rule out a compact arithmetic or Boolean circuit, a factorised
representation, a BDD/ZDD-style exact form, fixed-prior symbolic propagation,
purpose-specific moment compilation, or fixed-policy weighted model counting.

**The reframe this forces (§5.3, sharpened under DS-A21).** Matrix rank remains
a lower bound on linear factorisations and E5 says the relevant algebra
saturates. What changes is not the bound but the **target**: a solver that
separates an action never needs to represent every value function on the fiber;
it needs to evaluate finitely many inner products together with valid bounds.
Precisely: **predictive rank lower-bounds the corresponding linear factorisation
target; it does not by itself lower-bound the size of an unrestricted nonlinear
arithmetic or Boolean DAG.** Nothing here asserts that no lower-bound technique
applies to circuits — only that no result in this branch supplies one. This must
be stated as a change of target and **never as circuits beating a rank bound**;
those are different claims and only the first is available.

## 6. The parent's remaining theorems, with hypotheses moved into the statements

Each is the parent's claim restated so that the hypotheses its proof uses are
part of what it says.

- **Theorem E6.1 — width monotonicity (§6; audit T2).** For belief families
  typed on a **common world space** — a world is a full hidden deal, unchanged
  by play, and an observation refines the belief on that same space — the width
  is monotone in the family. **The typing clause is not cosmetic**: posteriors
  naturally live at successor interfaces over smaller fibers, so the comparison
  is well typed only in the deal-level view. On a proper subfamily the
  minimising envelope need not be unique, so W_reach is a well-defined number
  but "the reachable envelope" is not a well-defined set, and **nothing may be
  keyed to it**. At the full simplex the minimal envelope *is* unique and equals
  the uniquely-optimal-for-some-belief set, so **W_all = N_exp** — one object
  under two names, never two rows. *Superseded in part under DS-A23: the primary
  object is [Definition E9](walt-math-information-geometry.md#definition-e9--interface-local-reachable-decision-width).*
- **Theorem E6.2 — backward pruning (§6; audit T5).** Parent §6.3 proves
  monotonicity, which is necessary but **not sufficient**. The two additional
  facts, restated with proof so the errata is self-contained, are frontier
  reproduction and exactness of incremental pruning — Lemma G(2) and G(3).
  Clause (c), narrowed under DS-A26, records what pruning destroys.
- **Theorem E6.3 — value sandwich (§6; audit T7).** With nested candidate,
  lawful and relaxed classes evaluated under the **same α-map** — same field,
  mechanics, utility, count contract — at the **same belief** over the **same
  world set**, with no decimation inside either bound: L_a ≤ Q^H_B(a) ≤ U_a, and
  equal endpoints trap the middle.
- **Theorem E6.4 — root-action separation (§6; audit T8).** If L_{a⋆} ≥ U_a for
  every a ≠ a⋆ then a⋆ ∈ Opt^H(B); if every inequality is strict then Opt^H(B)
  = {a⋆}. **Caveat in the statement, not the commentary:** the non-strict form
  separates a **member** of the optimal set, not the set. Where the full optimal
  set is wanted the test is run per action, or the report says "contains a⋆".
- **Theorem E6.5 — finite adaptive gluing (§6; audit T9).** With each iteration
  adding an information equality satisfied by every H-policy and violated by at
  least one member of the current relaxation, plus **(G1)** each relaxed solve
  returns the exact maximum or a *proved* upper bound — proved per solver, not
  assumed — and **(G2)** the stopping test is the exposed-face criterion, which
  is a **search over the whole optimal face**: a negative answer requires proof,
  whereas an unlawful returned optimiser licenses only adding a cut. Then the
  upper witnesses decrease monotonically to Q^H and the procedure terminates.
  **The termination bound is the size of the initial relaxation** — doubly
  exponential — and **must never be read as a complexity claim**. A cut
  constrains the **relaxation**, never the lawful policy class and never the
  fiber; a "cut" applied to worlds is a declared exclusion remnant and carries
  none of these guarantees.

## 8.3. Lemma E7 — when dominance travels (DS-A25)

Let T be a bijection of latent domains with a bijection of the lawful policy
classes such that **α_{Tρ}(Tξ) = α_ρ(ξ)** for all ρ and ξ. Then (1) the
pointwise order corresponds, so Pareto frontiers correspond, the frontier count
transports, universal dominance transports, and decision-deadness transports;
(2) for every belief and its pushforward, the pairings agree, so optimality at a
belief, exposure and decision width transport **provided the belief is
transported too**.

**Binding form of the library rule.** *Dominance does not travel with a policy
alone.* A policy transport establishes only that the transported object is a
lawful policy at the new coordinate — which is what makes it a valid primal
witness, and is all that is needed for that. Transporting a **verdict** requires
the displayed identity, plus the belief for belief-relative verdicts.
[Corollary R-fold](walt-math-structure-transport.md#corollary-r-fold--the-predictive-dimension-is-declaration-fold-invariant)
is an instance and may be cited as one; **nothing else in the branch currently
is.**

## The candidate-policy library, and its one fatal failure mode

Three rulings govern it, and the first is a soundness rule, not an efficiency
one.

- **DS-A14.** Every L is produced by a fixed-policy evaluator satisfying the
  DS-A27 invariant, asserted **structurally, not by inspection**, plus L ≤ Q^H
  wherever H completes. If a candidate is priced by any world-informed
  evaluator, the number returned is an **upper** bound, the sandwich inverts,
  and the test separates the wrong action with a proof that looks valid.
- **DS-A15.** Seeds are heuristics for *finding* witnesses; witnesses are
  validated by exact evaluation, always. A library carries policies, not
  verdicts. "This policy was dominant at coordinate X" has no status at
  coordinate Y.
- **DS-A16, the one genuinely robust property.** Lower-bound validity depends
  only on a policy's **lawfulness**, not on the valuation under which it was
  discovered. So the library **survives count re-entry as a witness source**:
  every entry remains a valid primal witness under trick-plus-count, evaluated
  under that valuation, even though its count-free quality verdicts do not
  survive. This is the one place in the branch where count re-entry does not
  destroy prior work. **The policies extend; the verdicts do not.**

action-conditioned upper witness and its solver identification are
