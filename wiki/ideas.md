> **Epistemic tier: EXPLORATORY — below every tier on
> [Home](Home.md#evidentiary-tiers--never-promoted-never-blurred).** Everything
> reachable from this page sits below exchange-adjudicated CONFIRMED, below rob
> conformance receipts, and below corpus theorems. Nothing here is a result, an
> expectation, a schedule, or a receipt, and nothing here may be cited as one — not in
> a brief, not in a dispatch, not in FINDINGS. Ideas may quote proved facts as
> background; the idea itself never inherits the quoted fact's tier.

The purpose is capture, not advocacy: a place to write down a direction precisely
enough that it can be judged later, without it leaking into the parts of the wiki that
carry evidentiary weight. [open-problems](open-problems.md) records what the
mathematics leaves open; this page records what *we* have not yet earned the right to
claim. An idea leaves this page in exactly one of two ways — **promoted to a brief**
(it becomes an implementation assignment with named invariants and receipt rows), or
**promoted to a dispatch** (it becomes an adjudicated question on the exchange
channel). Anything that stalls stays here, or is deleted with its reason.

**Descendants (column added 2026-09-13; repository state as of 2026-09-07,
`c00717d1`).** A third thing can happen to an idea without it leaving this page: a
later object, built at its own tier — in practice walt's EXPLORATORY tier, on walt's
own basis (a declared field σ0, the pmake objective, counted belief) — takes the same
direction further. That object is a *descendant*. A descendant is a cross-reference,
never a promotion: the idea page stays uncited by anything above it, the idea's own
status column stays frozen at its last probe date, and a descendant's numbers are
quotable only from the descendant's own record or gate file, never from here. Each
idea page carries the same note in its own words (hierarchical fibers §11, retrograde
§9, seat-level context's build note).
