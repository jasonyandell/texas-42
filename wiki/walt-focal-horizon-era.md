## 5. The focal-horizon hierarchy (FH0–FH5)

**The object, plainly.** Take a root and one legal action. Below it, play a
fixed lawful policy (the "tail") and count the worlds it makes in — a
floor. Above it, let the seat see every hidden tile before each decision
(the "God" continuation) — a ceiling. Now make the first focal decision
exact: at the seat's next decision take the best action over every legal
tile at one public information state, and only below that fall back to the
tail (floor) or to clairvoyance (ceiling). Each further focal decision made
exact is one more unit of `k`. The interval `[L_{a,k}, U_{a,k}]` shrinks
as `k` grows and collapses to the exact value Q_a once `k` reaches the
number of focal decisions left, `h_f`. The **bar** is the best floor
`B_k = max_a L_{a,k}`; the **survivors** are the actions whose ceiling
clears it; the verdict is `Settled` (one survivor), `Equivalent` (every
survivor collapsed at the bar — the exact tie set, FH-tie) or
`Unresolved`; and `Γ_k = U*_k − V(π_k)` is the certified regret of the
materialized policy π_k. Public branches consume no horizon. Vocabulary
(FH-A2): **focal-horizon hierarchy** (the construction), **focal-horizon
interval** (the object at a node), **action interval**, **bar**, **survivor
set**, **focal depth `h_f`**; the parent's title word is not an object
name.

## 8. Method notes the book keeps

- **The independent audit is an instrument.** FH4 was run by an agent
  that built nothing on the branch (the standing audit-independence
  policy); its one BLOCK was a word, and its NOTEs found the one ungated
  correctness branch, the memory hazard and the number drift. UP0's audit
  caught a prose overclaim — "both flips pinned" when the gate carried one —
  and the remedy was the stronger one: extend the gate so the claim became
  true. The discipline recorded in UP0's deviation 7: *write the number
  into the gate first and the prose second, never the reverse.*
- **Gate, not prose.** The settling horizons were discovered by running,
  never pinned (FH-A8); "again in the gate" holds for 7 of 33 coordinates
  and the report says so (N5); numbers quoted from a record's width column
  were corrected to its split columns (N4).
- **The background-work wedge.** FH1's gate file and record were run in
  the background and the builder was never woken: 02:06 the builder
  yielded with both jobs running, 02:08 the record finished, 02:11 the gate
  suite finished (10 passed), nothing happened until the orchestrator's
  ping at about 07:15, commit 07:31 (CDT, 2026-09-04; FH1 deviation 5,
  `8efe6923`). It became `CLAUDE.md`'s Agents rule: never end a turn with
  background work pending.
- **The three-builder collision on MB0.** A harness false-drop spawned a
  third builder on the belief that the second had been killed; it had not
  (its files were advancing under live edits). The third builder's work
  survives as `MB0-COLLISION-NOTES.md`, a non-colliding audit record at
  scratch tier — and it is the record that first established the σ1
  hazard as pre-existing shipped code.
- **Vacuity typing.** A zero price at an endpoint (Q = 0 or Q = 1, or a
  fiber decided at the root) is not evidence; U0 typed it apart, MB1
  inherited the discipline unchanged and it changed a headline on first use
  (h12-t4). Any future Φ-censusing slice inherits it in the same form.
- **Refusals are measurements.** 78% of MB1's probe wall and 86% of U0's
  bought typed refusals at named places; the trick-3 question was posed as
  "does it close within the most a trick-4 coordinate cost?" so that a
  refusal is a located answer, not an absence.
