## The honest status

The architecture has passed its pre-code adjudication only in a narrowed form.
The first API is one exact opening-root model, not a generic public-state solver:
the focal seat is bidder, leader and actor; its seven-tile hand is known; the
other 21 tiles form the complete ordered 7/7/7 allocation support; auction
evidence is deliberately ignored; compatible deals have the uniform prior; the
field plays uniformly among legal actions; and utility is binary make/set for the
declaring team. Changing any of those choices creates a new profile and invalidates
shortcuts that depend on the present one.

Portable M0/M1 is source-complete for this bounded slice, and the canonical
run-envelope, grade-5 stop and regeneration path exist. The final checked source
manifest, committed comparands, fresh byte-for-byte regeneration, Rust gate and
Lean target passed together. The bounded status is therefore **PORTABLE M0/M1
COMPLETE under freeze 55**. Metal Gate 0 remains
**NO-GO on the current host** because a compatible full Xcode is not installed and
selected. M2–M5, the explicit perfect-recall information net, the stopped
controller and an opening-root verdict are untouched. This track has produced no
root action.

## What adjudication changed

Four repairs determine the implementation boundary.

1. **Narrow root.** The opening coordinate carries closed prior, evidence, field,
   utility, horizon and scale profiles. It is not a generic `PublicState` API.
2. **Current-trick points count.** A mid-trick decidedness bound includes count
   already lying in the unresolved trick; omitting it can declare a contract safe
   when it is set. The opening projector starts at a trick boundary, but later
   stages inherit the repaired invariant.
3. **Typed exact masses.** Support counts, likelihood coefficients, scaled cell
   masses, conditional values and weighted contributions are different roles.
   Arithmetic and comparison require compatible frames; no float enters the path.
4. **Exact information key.** A controller key must be extensionally equal to the
   canonical perfect-recall observation key. Adding an extra policy descriptor to
   that key can split one lawful information state and recreate strategy fusion.

The contract also distinguishes a non-strict optimal-member result from a playable
least-index action. Under the standing tie rule, play requires a unique optimum,
the exact optimal set, or the canonical inequalities that settle every lower-index
competitor strictly and every higher-index competitor non-strictly.

## What may be said

The present result is a serious exact foundation for the first build: a preserved
source, an adjudicated narrow contract, **PORTABLE M0/M1 COMPLETE under freeze
55** with a fully bound and integratedly checked reproducibility boundary, and a
limited Lean foundation. It is designed so Metal must reproduce canonical exact
objects rather than introduce its own semantics.

It is not yet a GPU solver result. Nothing here supports a claim about GPU speed,
opening strength, general opponent behavior, auction-aware belief, arbitrary
utilities, strategy-fusion elimination, root closure or a playable first lead.
Grade-4 checks are correctness instruments and are never evidence about trick 1.
