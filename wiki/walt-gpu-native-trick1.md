## What adjudication changed

Four repairs determine the implementation boundary.

1. **Narrow root.** The opening coordinate carries closed prior, evidence, field,
   utility, horizon and scale profiles. It is not a generic `PublicState` API.
2. **Current-trick points count.** A mid-trick decidedness bound includes count
   already lying in the unresolved trick; omitting it can declare a contract safe
   when it is set. The opening projector starts at a trick boundary, but later
   stages inherit the repaired invariant.
3. **Typed exact masses.** Support counts, likelihood coefficients, scaled cell
   masses, conditional values and weighted contributions are different roles.
   Arithmetic and comparison require compatible frames; no float enters the path.
4. **Exact information key.** A controller key must be extensionally equal to the
   canonical perfect-recall observation key. Adding an extra policy descriptor to
   that key can split one lawful information state and recreate strategy fusion.

The contract also distinguishes a non-strict optimal-member result from a playable
least-index action. Under the standing tie rule, play requires a unique optimum,
the exact optimal set, or the canonical inequalities that settle every lower-index
competitor strictly and every higher-index competitor non-strictly.
