## What the era is

The calculated-evidence era ended on a sampled instrument: anytime-valid
*estimates* with declared risk. This era replaces estimates with **exact
integer masses over the root fiber wherever they are affordable, and honest
typed refusals where they are not**. Jason's counted-belief sandwich parent
(hand factors, exact-cover partition functions, the §23 mass recursion cleared
of denominators) supplied the counting mathematics; its follow-on, the anytime
proof-state parent, supplied the epistemic container — an **append-only,
serializable proof state** whose facts are the only stored authority, whose
closure is a pure derived view, and whose deliverable is a **certified-regret
recommendation**: play this action, floor `B_exec`, at most `Γ = U* − B_exec`
unclaimed. Everything lands in-crate beside the existing solver, additively,
with the previous controller (`RefineV1`) semantically frozen as freeze 58 and
consumed only as a frozen oracle.

## What the era found

- **The opening-root verdict, in certified-regret form** (Phase 8, probe
  `openingreport`): at the receipt opening root h0-t1 (Z = 399,072,960,
  contract 30 on threes) the ladder p = 16/64/256/512 climbs the executable
  bar 0 → 732‰ while Γ falls 1000 → 267‰ and the recommendation migrates
  0-0 → 2-1 → 6-5. Verdict: honest UNRESOLVED at ε = 1/4 — *play 6-5, floor
  732‰, at most 267‰ unclaimed*. The **sampled tier plateaus at p = 512**; the
  remaining Γ is structural work, not more sampling.
- **The doom finding** (probe `doomreport`): where doom lives, the census
  harvests it wholesale — 809–1000‰ of the per-world truth on the enumerable
  receipt roots, one whole fiber at a single node. At the opening root it
  certifies an honest **zero**, and the diagnosis is the finding: hand-built
  crusher worlds and a declared 228-point grid all let the world-aware viewer
  make against σ0 after the 0-0 lead. A doom-family upper is floored at the
  God make rate, and that rate is ≈ 1 there — so the plateau's Γ ≈ 267‰ is
  overwhelmingly the **info-consistency price** of playing blind, purchasable
  by floor work and info-consistency-aware uppers, never by counterexample
  counting. The census's working domain is the endgame and in-play middlegame
  (t4–t6), where every played hand eventually arrives.
- **pmake belongs to the policy, not the first tile** (Phase 3/6): a settled
  best *action* can price below a rival's materialized *policy* until argmax
  extraction bridges §30 — observed, then closed exactly, on h3-t4.
- **Honest instrument behavior throughout**: refusals are typed and
  affordability-only once sampled facts exist; a poor forecast wastes budget
  but cannot weaken the proof state (§43); covers decline without an
  incumbent; first-generation covers are vacuous at rich roots (§70's caveat,
  live and recorded).
