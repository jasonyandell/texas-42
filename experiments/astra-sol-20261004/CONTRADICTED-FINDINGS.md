# Contradicted claims and correction history

This is an append-only intellectual history of specific failed claims, not a
list of ideas to abandon. Some claims came from retrieved drafts; others were
tempting shortcuts challenged during this effort. Unproved ambitions belong
in the open ledger, not here. Preserve the source packets and failed run logs.

1. **World-first optimization equals lawful imperfect-information optimization.**
   False in general: shared MAX=1 while world-first MAX=2 in the two-world Lean
   witness; shared MIN=1 while world-first MIN=0. `CompiledDAG.lean`. The Fable
   early C/Python kernels implement the relaxed order. A legal root chooser
   can use this heuristic but cannot call its continuation values lawful BR.
2. **Small inner value error/regret guarantees a small effect on an outer mind.**
   False without common-belief/action-stability conditions: the exact
   `ArgmaxAmplification.lean` family makes outer success fall 1→0 with inner
   regret approaching zero. The correction is action control or uniform
   payoff-disagreement transport under explicit coupling.
3. **Checking only the incumbent's replay is enough for a choice certificate.**
   False: unchanged A=[1,0], B=[0,0]→[1,1] changes the winning action while
   incumbent disagreement stays zero. `math/sol/witness.py` / final witness
   logs and phase2 `certificate_witness.py`. All competing continuations matter.
4. **An equal interval boundary always allows pruning.**
   False under least-tile ties when the competitor has smaller index. The
   canonical interval theorem and executable witness distinguish strict and
   weak exclusion. All intervals also require complete underlying work.
5. **The current substitution certificate is a demonstrated acceleration.**
   Contradicted on all four measured cold-fallback panels (C06). The proof is
   useful; this operationalization is not faster on these fixtures. This does
   not contradict cache-reusing fallback or another certificate strategy.
6. **The universal coordinate experiment avoids large unused preprocessing.**
   Contradicted on its own panel: 90.074% of coordinates unused across four
   tapes; cold one-bid cost 20.552× reference. The earlier 32.43× capacity/visit
   ratio is a different statistic and must not be called a union fraction.
7. **The adaptive 13-payoff speedup already establishes a fixed-contract gain.**
   Contradicted by the added single-bid test: cold adaptive 2.992× reference.
   The 0.632× repeated-payoff result remains accepted for its actual workload.
8. **Outer physical worlds necessarily cover an opponent's inner belief.**
   False: `InnerFiber.lean` proves omitted positive mass and a strict ranking
   reversal. Expanded fibers or external pinned field answers may repair this;
   universal mechanics over only the outer bundle cannot silently do so.
9. **Grouping public histories alone makes the recovered stochastic Python fold
   a correct expectimax.** False in general without cumulative reach weights.
   `archive_witness.py` gives 1 versus 2/3 weighted reversal where unweighted
   rows choose the other action. This is an abstract witness, not a concrete
   game failure. The C exact-tail fold carries weights correctly.
10. **The recovered original tiny-net validation/comparator is held-out-game
    validation against production Walt.** Contradicted by creation source:
    rows are randomly split; comparator is fused Dice. Later variants may
    differ and remain unverified. The original uniform label teacher itself
    is lawful; suggesting it must be fused merely because the simulator sees
    hidden hands would also be an incorrect diagnosis.
11. **The issue100 memo experiment is the tiny-net experiment.** Corrected by
    user clarification and source recovery: they share an idea but are distinct
    artifacts. The compiled Scheme fitting campaign is distinct again.
12. **Finite sampled 2048-rollout targets are exact population values, or
    iteration cycles imply no mixed Nash equilibrium exists.** Neither follows.
    Finite tape/bundle exactness is conditional; rock-paper-scissors already
    refutes the cycle→no-mixed-equilibrium implication. No equilibrium theorem
    for the complete partnership convention space is established here.
