Per-query sample microseconds truncate small intervals, and recursive lower times overlap. Neither counter establishes a causal cost partition.

Each tree now owns its own ordered index arena and a fixed17-ID retained descent stack. Expansions append ordered children. Nature parents update by subtracting the old child interval and adding the new one. MAX lower bounds and MIN upper bounds update monotonically; the opposite extremum rescans siblings only when the touched child had tied the prior extremum. Propagation stops when the parent's interval is unchanged. The immediate parent's active-child selection is refreshed even when its aggregate is unchanged; its ancestors then see an unchanged child and require no refresh. Root choice certification retains the original strict-earlier / weak-later inequalities and ascending tile order.

Root interval buffers and ancestry copies are fixed arrays. A completed tree commits its full-keyed choice before dropping its owned nodes/worlds/edges. Its charged rows/bytes are subtracted immediately. There is no retained free pool. Complete lower caches and completed trees survive subsequent refusal; prepared or unresolved trees do not enter the choice cache. The batch caller restores saved live accounting on success or error, while all local trees unwind on error. Sample order, complete ActorSpec cache identity, complete lower actor inputs and original root-vector fold are unchanged.
