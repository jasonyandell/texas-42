# Accepted and qualified ledger

Exploratory tier. The numbered [report](REPORT.md) and independent
[review](checks/REVIEW.md) supply premises and limits. Historical accepted,
qualified and contradicted evidence remains preserved at
[choice-arena](../choice-arena-20261004/REPORT.md),
[demand-bounds](../demand-bounds-20261004/REPORT.md),
[prefix-state](../prefix-state-20261004/REPORT.md),
[native-frontier](../native-frontier-20261004/REPORT.md) and
[adversarial](../adversarial-20261004/ACCEPTED.md).

- Preserved: exact ordered groups/full cache identity, finite demanded-call
  parity, prior demand savings and qualified support frontend cold gains.
- Constructive: resumable explicit paths, incremental touched-ancestor folds
  and immediate completed-tree release/cache insertions have finite parity
  evidence and conditional arithmetic laws.
- Qualified: component cost comparisons and a complete explicitly named local
  hybrid player are exploratory. A smoke head-to-head does not establish
  strength or phone-device latency.
- Open: generic higher-k linearity, useful policy acceleration over native,
  broad parallelism, and general equivalence of unrestricted strategies to
  shared precomputed plans. Existing schemes/tapes/coordinates/aggregation
  remain candidates, not an assumed theorem.
