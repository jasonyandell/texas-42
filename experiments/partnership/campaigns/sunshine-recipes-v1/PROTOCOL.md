# Continuation-selectable Scheme gym: validation protocol

2026-09-13. This experiment tests the scenario instrument and regenerates the
count-offer family with deployed L1 continuation. It does not change a player.

The accepted first deliverable is one existing recipe interface with selectable
continuation players, reusable query-independent action values, and comparisons
that retain counterexamples. The subsequent affordable online checker remains
a separate player change. The motivating skill can be rare in ordinary games;
its conditional competence is worth measuring directly.

## Fixed conditions

- Source: the checked-in `default-partner-battery` game records already used by
  the original Scheme family. This is a development corpus, not a new holdout.
- Coordinates: two or three tiles left in our own hand, bid 30 still unsettled,
  at least two legal choices; request seed 420600; limit 5,000 examined roots.
- Query: the existing `offer-count` Scheme expression, presence threshold 1.
  Its inputs and meaning do not depend on an action's answer-key value.
- Root belief: uniform mechanical support, capped at 400 compatible worlds.
  Every legal first action uses the same full support. No behavior weighting
  of earlier play and no actual source-deal hidden hand enters an actor request.
- Continuation: force each first move, then deployed `l1-default` at all four
  seats. First exact-input decisions are frozen with full configurations.
- Outcome: make/set only; all equal action values tie. Count points remain a
  diagnostic and never settle a grading tie.
- Ten parallel trajectories, two native threads per worker; existing 14-second
  player ceiling. Each run slice has a 295-second external watchdog.

## Checks

1. Interrupt a live deployed generation with SIGINT. Record hashes of all
   completed values, trajectories and cached decisions, resume, and verify that
   every saved byte survives. Partial generations must refuse gallery use.
2. Finish and independently audit the full collection: compatible-world support,
   lawful traces, own/public decision inputs, role configurations and make counts.
3. Recreate the original teacher-based family. Its reference checks the exact
   coordinate set and complete answer keys, not merely the count of 30.
4. Compare teacher and deployed values by stable coordinate ID. Preserve
   helpful, harmful and tied query relations in the discovery ledger.
5. Change selection to required offers, avoided offers, and a larger contrast;
   change the Scheme expression to five-count offers only. Verify applicable
   values and decisions stay unchanged, with no new deployed evaluations for
   query/filter variants confined to previously measured roots.
6. Compare the overlapping original targeted replay roots against this generic
   recipe's forced-action values. A changed ranking is reported, not hidden.

Unit tests cover preset validation, role selection, cache identity, failed-job
retry, sampler membership and information-local decision certificates. Sampling
is an optional instrument mode; the main experiment uses full support. Sampled
values must not claim a guaranteed outcome.

The outcome-selected collection is a diagnostic exercise set. It does not
estimate frequency in ordinary games, establish a new player's strength, or
make a model-independent claim about an action being correct.
