Execution includes the user-authorized shared pool, up to 10 simultaneous games across seeds. Per-game attempt receipts identify resumed moves and concurrency; deadline-dependent outcomes must be interpreted with the execution settings visible.

World completions are clustered by opening hand. The independent-deal sequential test is disabled; see WORLD-RESULTS.md for the conditional comparison.
