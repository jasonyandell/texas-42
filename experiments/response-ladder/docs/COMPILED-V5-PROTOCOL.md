# Separate leading and following programs

Prepared after the v4 role/order diagnostic and before v5 teaching or games.
The diagnostic used development data to choose the smaller lead/follow
representation; all subsequent outcomes remain development, not confirmation.
It improved same-target T0 and T1 local costs, while four-way trick-offset
partitioning worsened T1. This motivates an experiment, not a strength claim.

The new actor schema is `scheme-relational-lead-follow-v1`: `lead_clauses`
and following `clauses` each contain at most three distinct clause IDs from
the existing sixteen-clause vocabulary. The acting seat leads exactly when
the current public trick is empty. The actor receives no hidden information.
Legacy schemas retain their serialized form and behavior. Require independent
Scheme/scalar/fast-field and GPU trace contracts, including focal tails,
mixed legacy/new programs, phase fallback, role normalization and weighted
original scenario columns. A WASM library check establishes build portability
only, not browser or phone performance.

Fit role-specific C0 from the unchanged v4 n32 T0 corpus using training only.
Within each role, compute physical-group weights over all rows before selecting
the phase subsets. Do not give each phase a fresh normalization. Exact minimum
training cost selects each program, with fewer clauses then lexical order as
ties. Full role development scoring must exactly reproduce the diagnostic.

Freeze C0-v5, then generate new T1 labels against all seats using that C0.
Keep n1=8, original request IDs/groups/seeds/splits and 50ms query caps; retain
all complete, settled, refused and erroneous requests. Fit C1 with the same
lead/follow representation and weighting. Old v4 T1 labels remain a diagnostic
of capacity under the old field and cannot serve as this new C1 target.

The primary whole-game control uses CPU20, outer40, plans1, horizon7,
work2,000,000 and the compiled C1 focal tail, on all 72 mirrored pairs in
compiled-v1-dev001. Keep current Walt's ordinary 14s move allowance and six
threads. Complete all 144 games without outcome stopping, preserve failures
and fallbacks, independently replay, and report actual mean/p95 partnership
time alongside paired outcomes. Use 55s cycles under the existing 60s watchdog.
Do not combine this representation comparison with the separately running
outer80 control. A later GPU panel needs its own complete fixed configuration.

No fresh final holdout starts here. The goal remains stronger complete-game
play at comparable measured wall time; local teaching improvements do not
satisfy it.
