# Role and winning-tile representation control

Prepared before generation of the new labels and before its full-game outcomes.
This remains a development experiment; final confirmation has not started.

The frozen observation corpus is the original 72 physical calibration deals,
with the same 36 training and 36 development groups and both mirrored arms
kept together. The new campaign copies only its immutable request/manifest
files, with copy hashes in prepared-from.json. Old lessons remain unchanged.

C0 uses the named sixteen-clause grammar (v1 clauses plus unqualified and
partner-qualified least-legal tile that beats the current public winner).
Teach exact compatible-root T0 against Dice with n=8, a 50ms per-query deadline,
and all original bundle columns/tapes retained. Fit declaring and defending
programs independently over all 3,617 lists of at most three distinct clauses;
training cost alone selects, then shorter and lexicographically earlier lists.
Use equal source-group weight within each role and report both role costs.

Freeze the combined C0 role artifact before generating T1. T1 uses n=2 exact
compatible-root best responses against this role-aware frozen C0 at every
modeled seat, the same query deadlines and refusal reporting. Fit C1 with the
same role-aware sixteen-clause grammar. Do not reuse old T1 labels.

Scalar fast paths, strict Scheme exports, GPU traces and both-team teacher
contracts must pass before timing. Runtime opponents use role-specific C0;
partner and the lawful focal continuation use role-specific C1. The primary
online control keeps outer=40, plans=1, horizon=7, work=2,000,000, compiled_tail
true, and 20ms per move. Evaluate full CPU and GPU panels on the reused
compiled-v1-dev001 namespace, 72 mirrored pairs, current Walt unchanged with
six threads. Retain complete outcomes, fallbacks, failures and latency. Every
cycle uses 55s cooperative work and the existing 60s process-group watchdog.

The previous fourteen-clause pooled C0/C1 with learned focal continuation is
its existing control: 4 wins, 6 losses, 62 ties on both backends. This experiment
changes representation and role structure together; it does not identify their
individual causal contributions. Local teacher cost is not whole-game regret.

A later separately named teaching control may increase n0/n1 to reduce teacher
sampling noise at unchanged online execution cost. Those labels target larger
finite bundles, and cannot be called the same exact labels or retroactively
substituted in this campaign. Any such control requires new C0 and C1 artifacts.
