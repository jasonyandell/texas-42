# Outcome-first mining: fresh replication and relational lifting

Exploratory protocol frozen 2026-09-18, before the fresh labels are inspected.
The sunshine objective remains a fast, reasonable, partner-aware player. These
probes assess an outcome-pattern mining instrument; they do not alter the player.

## Two probes, one fresh evaluation

1. Replicate the eight v1 literal hypotheses and the post-hoc simpler `before
   holds 5-5` hypothesis on new hands/worlds. The latter is the literal primary.
2. Derive relational descriptions from losing discovery worlds, replacing a
   physical hidden tile by an existential role with mechanical properties and
   optionally a beating relation to an own-hand lead. The top discovery-ranked
   relational query is the second primary. Retain four diverse relational
   candidates; never select or retune using the fresh sample.

Discovery remains v1's first 15 source deals / trials 0..3: 2,160 games. The
remaining v1 data is now development evidence, not fresh confirmation. No new
outcome labels enter generation or ranking. The original v1 predictions and
negative results remain intact.

## Relational grammar (v2.1)

Generate descriptions of actual hidden tiles in losing discovery examples.
Holder is one of the three chairs relative to the bidder. Each description
retains zero, one, or two true tile properties from:

- `double(d)` or its negation;
- `count(d,0|5|10)`;
- `in(d,q*)` (called/trump membership) or its negation;
- `boss(d,q*)` (top live called follower) or its negation.

Optionally retain an observed `beats(d,own,led)` relation, with
`own-legal(own)` and `leads-context(own,led)`. For that own tile, retain either
no further property, called membership, or nonmembership. This is a static
beating relation, not a claim the hidden holder can legally play d at that
future turn or ultimately capture the trick.

Each clause has ONE hidden tile role, and at most ONE own tile role. Their
ownership makes them distinct. There is no accidental distinct-witness
requirement from combining separately existential features. Clauses are single
conjunctions, with no specific tile literals, negated existentials, invented
tactical predicates, history, scores, or outcome labels. Properties are selected
mechanically from source examples; the learned description is not supplied as a
prewritten tactic. Some property combinations may still identify one physical
tile; report that honestly rather than calling every query a broad abstraction.

Use the same 64 losing discovery worlds selected in v1. Deduplicate clauses.
Require 36 matching discovery games and six source deals. Rank by the same
positive squared within-cell failure excess / matches as v1, with fewer atoms
and canonical description as ties. Keep four candidates with pairwise coverage
Jaccard <4/5. Freeze source descriptions, seed identities, complete candidate
library, ranking counts, and query files before reading fresh labels.

## Fresh data and statistical gate

- Original deal seeds **420625..420634**, four bidder hands each, all nine
  declarations, trials **0..7**: 2,880 full games / 320 hidden completions.
- The same deployed bid30 profile and pinned worker binary as production.
  Complete all seven tricks. No screening, conditional-world selection, or
  optional stopping based on results. This finite campaign is separate from
  the active bidding campaign and reuses its durable queue/receipt machinery.
- Reuse a game only if its complete player/request/seed identity matches. Keep
  the fresh corpus quarantined from learner selection until the fitted artifact
  is immutable. Aggregate progress is not a selection signal.
- Report matched failures, same-cell baseline, excess, coverage, all controls
  and reversals. Shuffle trial outcome vectors jointly across declarations
  within each bidder hand, as in v1, with 1,999 fixed-seed permutation draws.
- The two primaries are fixed in advance. A primary meets this exploratory
  replication gate if excess is >=3 percentage points, matches include >=20
  hidden completions and >=5 source deals, and its one-sided permutation p is
  <=.025 (Bonferroni for two primaries). This is a research gate, not a theorem
  or a guarantee of player improvement. Report max-statistic adjusted values
  for all 13 queries as a separate secondary audit.
- Check every learned candidate using the actual Scheme engine, including
  training and fresh inputs. Cache membership independently of outcome labels.
  Audit the mechanical Python matcher against Rust on the generated library's
  source examples, all declarations, and chair rotations. Refuse publication
  on any mismatch.

## Decision after the probes

A replicated primary supports investing in a reusable mining rig: immutable
datasets and splits, bounded grammars, persisted candidate Schemes and source
witnesses, ablation lineage, independent replication, and retained failures.
The bounded fit/evaluate tools built for these probes are its first components.
A failure is retained and confines claims to this grammar/sample/continuation.
Do not quietly expand the grammar or sample after looking at results.

Replication establishes an outcome association under this player. The next gate
toward sunshine is practical value: hold policy randomness or worlds fixed to
investigate mechanism, then test cheaper decision estimates or partner-aware
continuations at equal budgets. Hidden-world descriptions remain analysis/belief
queries, never illicit inputs to a player's current decision.
