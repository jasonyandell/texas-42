# og-v2: the constructor opened the language, and the exam nearly doubled og-v1

2026-09-21. **EXPLORATORY** finite-target evidence; every number is about
the declared campaign target only — never a strength claim against L0/L1
fields, human tables, other contracts, or the auction. Machine-readable
records govern this prose: `generations.jsonl`, `exam.json`, `panel.json`
(the versioned dedup panel over the final 38-expression dictionary),
`state.txt` (the final incumbent: every learned expression as a canonical
s-expression, every coefficient an exact power of 9/8).

## What changed from og-v1

og-v1 froze the dictionary at the human-versioned 14-clause seed library —
a control, exactly as the parent's §5 warns ("current three-clause programs
are controls, not an a priori ceiling on the autonomous constructor").
og-v2 runs the constructor: a fixed typed language of **539 bounded
expressions** over the scheme viewer predicates (22 action fragments —
double/master/count-n with negations, follow/boss/would-lead of the led
context, trump membership and trump-boss via the `Called` literal, and
`takes-trick`: action beats the current winning tile — × 13 public-record
qualifiers — partner/opponent currently winning, four trick positions,
hand-size stage markers — plus action-fragment pairs). Every expression
compiled and text-round-trip verified; the language and caps were declared
before the run; no human selected any relation. Per generation: dedup and
constancy filtering on the versioned panel (empirical agreement recorded as
NOT semantic equivalence), candidate derivatives g_F at coefficient zero on
a fresh frozen-incumbent bundle (parent §3's covariance score), top-3
admitted at weight 1 (provably inert until the gradient moves them), cap 60.

Same target law as og-v1; every seed base shifted by the campaign offset
100,000,000, so **no deal is shared with og-v1**. Same evidence rule
(δ = 1/20, τ = 1/100, checkpoints to 65,536 paired deals).

## What the constructor found (state.txt governs; largest movers)

- `x[double|pos-lead]` rose to (9/8)^23 ≈ ×15 — **lead your doubles**.
- Seed `partner-winning-count-5` rose to (9/8)^21 ≈ ×11.9 and learned
  `x[not-count0|partner-winning]` to ≈ ×4.1, while seed
  `partner-winning-count-0` fell to (8/9)^24 ≈ ×0.06 — **give count, not
  blanks, to partner's winning trick**.
- Seed `master` rose to ≈ ×4.6 while `partner-winning-master` fell to
  ≈ ×0.11 — **spend masters only where they buy something**.
- `x[not-double&not-master]` fell to (8/9)^7 ≈ ×0.44 — ordinary tiles are
  the default sacrifice, not the default play.
- `x[not-count5&takes-trick]` moved up and `x[double&loses-trick]` down
  (exact values in `state.txt`) — trick-taking geometry entered through the
  constructed `beats` composite, which no seed clause could express.

These are readings of exact coefficients, not adjudicated 42 strategy.

## Caveats

- Same target fence as og-v1: random-legal co-players, longest-pip
  declaration, bid-30 make/set. Transfer to stronger lineups is a new
  campaign (the natural next one: L0-8 opponents, then the gym field, with
  the multifidelity harness reusing cheap players as control variates).
- Candidates 3, 5, 6, 8 remain UNRESOLVED (real positive means below the
  resolvable band); the CE anytime-valid machinery would likely resolve
  them — intake companion agenda item 6.
- The g_F scores collected on the moving working weights are proposal
  statistics only; admissions never spent risk (weight-1 admissions are
  play-invisible), and every promotion consumed only exact make/set counts.
- ~2.6M complete deals total, ≈ 40 min single-threaded.
