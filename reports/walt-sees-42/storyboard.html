Walt rolls dice.

An edutainment short for people who already keep support and belief in separate boxes. The reveal: under all the machinery, walt's seat is a random process. Take the hand you have and what you've seen, ask what could be there, roll uniform dice for everyone else, count, then stack that loop on itself.

The spine, in one breath

What I hold + what I've seen → what could be there (support) → draw a world → everyone else rolls a fair die over their legal tiles → play it out → one bit: make or set → count → P(make). Then put that whole loop in the other chairs and do it again.

2 · "How it stacks up": the ladder, the scoreboard, or both?

Draft 1 does both: shot 10 stacks dice → level 0 → level 1 → level 2, and shot 11 shows the 5-5 tally. An arena result against the E[Q] champion could close the act, but I'd pull its exact numbers from arena_results_2026-08-17.txt before putting them on screen.

3 · How hard do we lean on the ideology joke?

Shot 3 has a soapbox pundit trying to sell walt a belief ("Opp L ALWAYS trumps in!"). It could be one beat, or a recurring heckler who gets refuted each act.
