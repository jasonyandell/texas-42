## Q1 arguments

P1. Trick::points() = 1 + captured count; seven tricks bank exactly 42; banked totals are monotone nondecreasing. banked_T1 >= 30 forces the indicator to 1 at every terminal below (value exactly 1, posterior-independent); banked_T0 >= 13 forces final T1 = 42 - final T0 <= 29 (value exactly 0). banked_T0 = 12 stays live and the code's strict "> 12" is correct. Totality bonus the code relies on (its own Lean row): at any terminal T1+T0 = 42 forces one cutoff to fire, so the assert at ladder.rs:402 is unreachable-safe and the recursion is total with no explicit terminal case.

P2. Values are conditional probabilities in [0,1] (cutoffs return 0/1; viewer nodes max; field nodes take convex combinations — the mass-conservation assert witnesses the weights sum to 1). A child of exact value 1 makes an S1 max exactly 1; unscanned siblings cannot exceed 1. BigRational comparison and is_one() are exact. The root report (run_boundary) scans every lead with a full solve and no root-level early exit, so per-lead values, argmax, and tie set are exact.

P3. The continuation is generated entirely by data recoverable from (played, leader, in-trick plays, banked pair, posterior id) given the fixed world list: actor = (leader + |plays|) mod 4; led context from plays[0]; legal sets from hands = mask & !played; trick resolution from (leader, four plays); the payoff is the indicator of current banked_T1 + future increments >= 30, and future increments depend on future play only. Equal reduced keys root identical continuation problems, so the memo (shared across the five root leads) is sound. Note: banked_T0 is redundant in the key (derivable from played, plays, banked_T1) — harmless. Under trick-differential the terminal reads accumulated tallies not in the key — unsound there, as stated.

P4. Lemma: V(R, c·w) = V(R, w) for integer c >= 1, so V factors through the primitive vector w/gcd(w); two positive integer vectors proportional over Q have equal primitive forms, so interning merges exactly the proportional posteriors. Induction: cutoffs constant; viewer children share the posterior (S1's play is measurable w.r.t. its own information — carries no evidence; code passes alive unchanged); at field nodes scale = lcm{k_w} depends only on the support (unchanged by scaling), every bucket, reach, and mass scale by c, ratios reach/(scale·mass) are invariant, and child buckets keep their primitive form. Implementation survives: bucket vectors stay in ascending world-ordinal order (order-preserving subsequences of the ascending uniform vector), so structural Vec equality = weight-function equality; all weights stay positive, so support = alive set. Caveat: worst-case weight growth (up to 420 per field move) can exceed u128 at t <= 2 (420^15 ~ 10^39 > 2^128); verified overflow-checks = true in ALL profiles (walt/Cargo.toml:67-74), so the failure mode is a panic, never a wrong value — expect it as a possible death mode distinct from the wall clock on deep runs.

Bonus counterexample (both branches coexist in one solve tree as S1's two lead orders at one info state — exactly the states P3 would wrongly merge without the posterior key). Trump fives; effective suits: sigma2 = {20,21,22,32,42,62}, sigma3 = {30,31,32,33,43,63}; 32 lies in both. Fragment hands (S1 to lead, 3 tiles each): S1 {22,33,55}; S2 {32,63,T}; S3 {62,61,44}; S0 {W,43,10}; world A: T=66, W=30; world B: T=30, W=66. Trick X: S1 leads 22; S2 32, S3 62, S0 sloughs 10; S1's double wins, +6 T1. Trick Y: S1 leads 33; S2 63, S3 sloughs 61, S0 43; S1 wins, +1 T1. Both orders are legal positive-probability records in both worlds and end at the identical reduced key (same mask, leader S1, banked +7 T1, empty in-trick). Field-likelihood products: order (X,Y): A = 1·1·(1/3)·1·(1/2)·(1/2) = 1/12, B = 1/12 → odds 1:1. Order (Y,X): A = (1/2)(1/3)(1/2)·1·1·(1/2) = 1/24, B = (1/3)(1/3)(1)·1·1·(1/2) = 1/18 → odds 3:4. Non-proportional. Mechanism: 32 counts toward S2's legal set in both contexts while in hand, and S0's follow/void status in suit 3 depends on whether 43 already left its hand — the path-ordered product of 1/k factors does not factor into (world part)×(order part). Orders whose k-profiles match up to a world-independent constant DO merge, and P4's interning catches exactly those.

## Q2 — the quotient

Invariance lemma (the concrete §12.6A instance the ladder should quotient by). State X = (R, beta), R = (played, leader, in-trick plays, allowance), beta primitive posterior. Let pi permute tiles and theta permute effective contexts such that, on the live universe: (1) pi fixes every played tile and every tile of S1's remaining hand pointwise; (2) pi(sigma_q ∩ live) = sigma_theta(q) ∩ live for every context q; (3) led(pi t) = theta(led t), and theta fixes the current in-trick led context; (4) trick-key order is preserved: tau(pi t, theta q) > tau(pi t', theta q) iff tau(t,q) > tau(t',q) for all live t,t'; (5) count(pi t) = count(t). Then V(R, beta) = V(R, pi_#beta) and Q(R, beta, s) = Q(R, pi_#beta, s) for every S1 action s (fixed by pi) — pmake value and argmax set preserved tile-for-tile. (Clauses 1+2 automatically preserve S1's legal sets.) Proof shape: pi induces a continuation-tree isomorphism (field plays transported, S1 plays fixed); (2)-(3) give world-wise legal-set equivariance, hence equal k's and equal likelihood factors — this is condition (ECL); (4) preserves winners; (5)+winners preserve banked increments and the indicator. Conclusion 7 of the §12.6A theorem (V_quot = V_exact, Q_quot = Q_exact) applies. The payoff is not count-free, so this is the theorem's tile-feature role re-entry form: counts and key-order are declared domino roles the transports must preserve — clauses (4)-(5). This makes the question's "no reachable continuation distinguishes" precise: incidence pattern + key-order position + count, not suit membership alone.

Relation to M1/M2 cells: a canonical cell mixture is a lossless encoding of the exact posterior — the relation it induces is posterior equality, i.e. strictly finer than the lemma's quotient. Cells are the carrier; the lemma's groupoid acts on it; the right object is the composition (canonicalize cells, then merge transport-equivalent shapes — K-OPEN-6 upgraded from "identical" to "equivariant"). What cells change is that state size stops scaling with world count: conditioning a uniform cell on an observed field action splits it by exact legal-count strata into a finite mixture of uniform cells (guide §6.2 — the same 420/k mechanism the ladder applies world-by-world, applied shape-wise). The t=1 root is ONE cell; the 399M-world materialization wall and its ~6GB disappear.

Honest negative result: on this carrier the lemma's group is nearly trivial. Counts pin 50,41,32,55,64; S1's hand pins contexts 1,2,3 and called; candidate suit swaps die (0<->6 on 64 being a 10-count, 0<->4 on 41 and 64; within-suit swaps like 20<->30 break on sigma2/sigma3 incidence that S1's own 21/31/33 keep live). Checked directly on the t=4 pool: no nontrivial (pi, theta) survives. Predicted classes among the 139k t=4 posteriors: ~139k (compression < 2x, likely ~1x) — conjectural pending a probe, but the group computation is hand-checkable. §12.6A earns its keep across carriers and in late endgames where contexts die, not on this single mid-hand walk.

Coarser under pmake? Yes, in the key, not the posterior: the banked pair collapses to the T0-allowance automaton state a = 12 - banked_T0 in {0..12} ∪ {busted} (the M3 contract's loss automaton); make iff future T0 spend <= a, and banked_T1 is derivable from (played, plays, a). One <=14-valued coordinate where trick-differential needs the exact tally pair. Negligible on the frozen t=4 carrier (banked pinned 7/1, P1 eats clamped regions); modest but free on a walked solve. The posterior partition is objective-independent.

Class counts at the t=1 root of hand 8. S1's seven leads occupy only four led contexts (called for 52/54/55; natural 1, 2, 3) — §5.1 reuse applies. Provable now: trick-2 boundary <= 7 × 21·20·19 = 55,860 public records, each with exactly one path (path-dependence needs two reorderable field moves by one seat), hence one posterior each, each a mixture of <= 10 opening cells — trivially feasible iff cell-carried. Extrapolating (conjecture only): 10^7-10^9 belief states at mid boundaries, 10^10+ memo nodes for the full walk — 2-4 orders of magnitude past a single-threaded exact-rational HashMap, and exactly wavefront-over-cell-frontier shaped.
