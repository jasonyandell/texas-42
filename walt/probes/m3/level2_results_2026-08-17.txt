Verdict
-------
Level-2 agrees with level-1 at every rung: 3-3 at t=4 and t=3, 5-5 at the
opening. A walt that models its partner as a coordinating level-1 mind still
pulls trumps with the master at trick 1. On this carrier the qualitative
shift does not change the line — which is itself evidence: the level-1 line
was already partner-compatible here. (One carrier, one hand; says nothing
yet about hands where coordination is load-bearing, e.g. finesse or duck
lines. Cross-carrier ladder is the natural next probe, and the parallel
solver prices it.)

Saturation note: 55 at 3200/3200 is support-on-sample, not certainty
(support != belief). The n=200 tie and its break at n=800 is the cleanest
demonstration yet that saturation ties are artifacts to be refined, never
broken by index order. level2.rs deliberately has NO tie refinement (pure
probe); any promoted use inherits the adaptive refinement from
playout/bridge.

Correction history (the parallel port caught a real bug)
--------------------------------------------------------
The serial probe's PiKey — documented as the modeled mind's "entire
information state" — omitted the banked totals, which the mind's pmake
objective conditions on and which are NOT derivable from the reduced record
(played mask + current-trick plays + leader do not determine the banked
split). Serial execution hid this as deterministic first-come cache
aliasing; the parallel port made the alias racy and therefore visible
(values drifted a few worlds between runs, which exact arithmetic forbids).
Fix: PiKey carries banked_t1/banked_t0; every cache entry is a pure function
of its key; parallel results verified byte-identical across 1 thread, 18
threads, and reruns (t=4). Argmax unchanged at every rung; values moved by
up to ~1.7 points (t=4 lead 31: 85.83% -> 87.50%).

Serial (aliased) ladder for the record: t=4 33 at 96.75% (21 92.75, 31
85.83, 55 93.00), 13.9s; t=3 33 at 96.40% (11 96.20, 21 95.00, 31 87.80,
55 95.60), 74.8s; t=2 ~100%, 27.8s; t=1 n=200 tie 11/31/55, 290.7s; t=1
n=800 55 unique at 800/800 (11 99.75, 21 99.87, 31 99.00, 33 99.75,
52 98.62, 54 99.50), 1211.3s. Same argmax at every rung as the corrected
ladder.

The SAME latent aliasing exists in level1.rs, playtable.rs, webtable.rs and
walt_bridge.rs (their pi0 caches). Those numbers stand as what those serial,
deterministic solvers computed; the fix is DEFERRED there until the 3x384
arena protocol completes, so seeds 1-3 play the identical bridge. Apply the
one-struct change (banked fields in PiKey) everywhere afterwards.

Costs (18 threads, 6P+12E, concurrent with a running arena match): t=1
n=800 214.9s vs 1211.3s serial (~5.6x); t=4 3.1s vs 16.0s single-threaded
on the corrected build. Modeled-mind solves are ~35us pure-function leaf
tasks under a sharded concurrent cache — the same factoring as the GPU
escape hatch, when that word comes.
