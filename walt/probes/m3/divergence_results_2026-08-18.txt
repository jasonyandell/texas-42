Caveats and next steps
----------------------
- Self-graded: every "divergent" verdict is level-2's own table. The
  referee protocol: replay divergent hands mirrored (level-1 vs level-2 in
  the shadow chair, all else identical), McNemar on the discordant pairs —
  all signal, no agreement-noise. Not yet run; needs Jason's sense of
  whether to spend the compute.
- n=40 estimator noise inflates the base rate; treat sub-500bp divergences
  as unresolved ties, not disagreements.
- Trajectories are level-1 self-play: positions level-2 would have steered
  into are unexplored (mining bias, acknowledged).
- Level-tag caveat: the modeled partner reads the shadow as a LEVEL-0
  mind (a simpler self) — signaling into a slightly wrong reader; the
  divergences found are a lower bound on what matched-reader coordination
  would surface.
