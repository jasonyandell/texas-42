level-2 ladder results — 2026-08-17

EXPLORATORY tier. Sampled values are ESTIMATES, never receipts; nothing here
is a P-A21 statement. Probe binary: src/bin/level2.rs (commit 178722e).

The question (Jason, 2026-08-17): "at one level deeper, a player's concept of
another player will be a level 1 walt. a level 1 walt has partnership that a
level 0 doesn't... level 2 is the qualitative shift." Level-2 is the first
level whose model of the partner coordinates back. Does that change the play?

Frozen carrier: receipt hand 8, trump fives, P30 by T1, S1 = viewer with
initial hand {5-2,5-4,1-1,2-1,3-1,3-3,5-5}. Stack: S1 best-responds over
n_outer sampled worlds to a field of LEVEL-1 minds (each best-responding over
its own n1=8 no-void belief to a level-0 field of n0=4-world minds over dice).
No seat at any level conditions on hidden cards — no strategy fusion anywhere.
pi_cache is cross-level; keys carry a level tag (0 for k=0, keeping k=0
bit-identical with level1.rs).

Verdict
-------
Level-2 agrees with level-1 at every rung of the ladder: 3-3 at t=4 and t=3,
5-5 at the opening. A walt that models its partner as a coordinating level-1
mind still pulls trumps with the master at trick 1. On this carrier the
qualitative shift does not change the line — which is itself evidence: the
level-1 line was already partner-compatible here. (One carrier, one hand;
says nothing yet about hands where coordination is load-bearing, e.g. finesse
or duck lines. Cross-carrier ladder is the natural next probe.)

Saturation note: 55 at 800/800 is support-on-sample, not certainty
(support != belief). The n=200 tie and its break at n=800 is the cleanest
demonstration yet that saturation ties are artifacts to be refined, never
broken by index order. level2.rs deliberately has NO tie refinement (pure
probe); any promoted use inherits the adaptive refinement from
playout/bridge.

Costs: level-2 t=1 n=800 is ~20 min single-threaded — vs level-1's ~1 min at
n=2000. The piper (n1*n0 inner worlds per pi call) is real but the cross-level
pi cache holds: 34.8M pi evaluations at t=1 n=800 collapsed to cache hits.
Bridge integration is feasible if a cross-carrier ladder justifies it.
