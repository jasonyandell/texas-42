Quick mechanics refresher so this note stands alone. Pips {0..6}; the 28 dominoes are the edges-with-loops of K7. Four seats, opposite seats partnered, seven tricks. A declaration (pip-trump throughout this note) fixes the called suit; a led tile sets a context; you must follow the context if you can; trick key is lexicographic (called beats follower beats slough, doubles top of their natural suit); unique max wins and leads next. Our working valuation here is count-free: the focal team's trick differential. The seat we model sees only its hand and the public record; behind a 7-tile hand at trick 1 sit C(21,7)·C(14,7) = 399,072,960 possible worlds. The declared belief is uniform over that set and the other three seats are a fixed uniform-random-legal field — a declared model, nobody's actual psychology.

Where your architecture stands, all of it machine-verified this week, most of it in the last twenty-four hours:

What replaced it is lovely: a **drawing-hand theorem**. If your trumps are the top-heavy majority of the suit (top-run, at least 4 of the 7) and everything else in your hand is a natural double, then "draw trumps, then run the doubles" takes all seven tricks in every world against *every* field behaviour.
