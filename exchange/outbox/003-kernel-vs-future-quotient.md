## 1. Game mechanics (compressed but exact)

Pips P = {0..6}; dominoes D = the 28 multisets {i,j} over P, written h:l with h >= l; doubles p:p; sigma_p = { d : p in d }. Count labels c(d): 5 for 5:0, 4:1, 3:2; 10 for 6:4, 5:5; else 0. Four seats Z/4Z clockwise, opposite seats partners, each dealt 7 tiles; an auction (hand-independent legality) fixes a bidder who declares delta in {0..6 (pip trump), DT (doubles), NT (no-trump)} and leads trick 1.

Called set kappa_delta = sigma_p | the 7 doubles | empty. Contexts 0..6 and 7 (called suit); effective suits sigmahat_q = sigma_q \ kappa_delta (q in P), sigmahat_7 = kappa_delta; led context ell(d) = 7 if d called else high(d); follow F(d,q) = 1 iff d in sigmahat_q. Leader plays anything (fixing q); a follower must follow if able, else sloughs freely. Trick key of d in context q: (tier, rank) lexicographic; tier 2 iff d in kappa_delta (never in NT), tier 1 iff uncalled and F(d,q) = 1, else 0; rank = double index under DT, TOP for doubles otherwise, pip sum for mixed. Unique max wins the trick and leads next; trick reward = 1 + sum of counts in it; seven tricks.

Viewer support. Fix viewer m, hidden seats h_1,h_2,h_3 = m+1,+2,+3 clockwise. After a legal prefix: hidden pool U = D \ (viewer hand union played); capacities k_s; voids V_s (contexts where s publicly sloughed); cells P_s = U \ Union_{q in V_s} sigmahat_q; fiber Phi = disjoint capacity-respecting triples covering U (exactly the possible hidden hands: losslessness, Math 7.5). Support normal form N (Math 7.10): certain tiles K_s (marginally held only by s), ambiguous pool W, residuals r_s, tag Determinate/Binary(inactive, W, split)/Ternary(W, r_0, r_1, exclusion map epsilon: W -> seats, epsilon(d) = s iff every world keeps d away from s only). N is a complete invariant of Phi (CELL-14). Typed transitions (Math 7.14.1, TRANS-08/09): given N, delta, actor, tile, and led context/lead boundary, the successor N' is uniquely determined (matching-minor calculus: force possession edge, delete slough-forbidden edges, contract played tile, reduce); viewer plays leave N unchanged; a hidden observation is accepted iff the conditioned successor fiber is nonempty.

## 2. The reduced viewer kernel (Math 7.16)

Current-trick fold. For declaration delta and led context q let C_{delta,q} be the nonzero-tier tiles ordered by trick key, and ord(d) their increasing ordinal (|C_{delta,q}| <= 13). An unresolved trick folds to chi = (q, r, w, z): led context, ordinal of the current maximum key (0 if only tier-0 tiles so far — note the leader always has positive tier so r >= 1 once led), winning seat w, pending count z in {0,5,10,15,20,25}. New play (s,d): r_d = ord(d) if d has nonzero tier else 0; replace (r,w) by (r_d,s) iff r_d > r; add c(d) to z. Theorem (fold congruence): given remaining hands or exact support, delta, and actor order, equal (q,r,w,z) implies identical future follow legality, winner updates, final trick winner and trick reward for every common legal continuation.

Actor/leader recovery (Math 7.16.2): during an open trick the four remaining-hand sizes take exactly two adjacent values and the low seats form one clockwise interval; the leader, plays made, and next actor are all determined. At a trick boundary all sizes are equal and the next leader must be stored explicitly.

Kernel. For a fixed contract, viewer frame, and named utility accumulator interface, the reduced exact viewer play/support kernel is

with H_m the viewer's remaining hand, N the support normal form, tau either the boundary leader or the folded open trick (q,r,w,z), and alpha_U the utility accumulator (exactly the utility residue not represented as transition reward; possibly trivial). Theorem PLAY-17 (proved): K determines viewer legal actions, acceptance and exact support transition for every public hidden action, actor order, trick winner and reward, terminal detection, and the accumulator transition. So K is an EXACT sufficient state.

## 3. Future equivalence (Math 12.10)
